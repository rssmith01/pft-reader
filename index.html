New evidence-first engine; measured-test scope; independent lung-volume observations; no automatic disease-specific diagnoses; report wording adapted from the ATS standardized phraseology (MacIntyre et al. 2025); a descriptive all-normal conclusion; Tests performed selector and spirometry-only normal preset; Next required shortcut; concise flow-volume-loop lines; FEV1 grading of obstruction when FVC and/or TLC is normal, with ungraded obstruction and a coexisting-restriction caveat when FVC is low without usable TLC; possible-obstruction wording for a low ratio with high FVC; effort/weakness, high-BMI, concave-loop, hemoglobin-effect, VA/KCO/VA-TLC and overall quality-grade options; amber attention highlights and matching Checks; copying blocked only by internal-consistency checks (no acknowledgement step); one-click severity modifiers; borderline-low buttons; Normal spirometry button; Hb-aware quality buttons; statement-only unusable-spirometry report; PRISm only when TLC is unavailable; complex-restriction (RV/TLC) wording; automatic air-trapping/hyperinflation statements; new patient scrolls to the top; concise quality; guided report modules; isolated teaching cases. No patient report is persisted. Revert by opening your saved previous HTML; this file does not modify it. Custom preset schema is version-isolated.

Interpretive framework: ERS/ATS 2021 standard, published 2022. Quality: ATS/ERS spirometry 2019, ERS/ATS DLCO 2017. Original source recommendations are dated; use current lab-approved reference equations supplied by your PFT system. This app does not compute GLI predictions or Hb corrections.

How the logic works

    
    
      
  • Findings are never pre-selected. Unmarked sections are left out of the report and flagged under Checks.
    • 
      
  • Interpretation rows tagged auto follow your findings live. Choosing an option yourself marks it manual ↺; click the tag or press 0 to hand it back to auto.
    • 
      
  • Patterns follow the ERS/ATS 2021 algorithms: obstruction = FEV1/FVC < LLN; restriction = TLC < LLN; mixed = both; complex restriction = low TLC with raised RV/TLC or FRC/TLC and a normal FEV1/FVC; low FVC/FEV1 with a normal ratio and no low TLC = nonspecific pattern/PRISm.
    • 
      
  • Report wording is adapted from the standardized phraseology of MacIntyre NR, et al., Ann Am Thorac Soc 2025;22(11):1633-1640 (doi:10.1513/AnnalsATS.202501-063PS). Your own template sentences (PRISm, complex-restriction explanation, DLCO differential, bronchodilator comment, early-restriction and full-PFT statements) remain custom.
  • A fully normal study names what was measured and found normal (for example "Normal spirometry, static lung volumes and diffusing capacity."). Use "Tests performed" (or ⇧O / ⇧S) for a spirometry-only study.
  • Mild ↓ / Moderate ↓ / Severe ↓ buttons on FVC, FEV1, FEV1/FVC, TLC and DLCO select the finding and its modifier together. Cutoffs: mild z −1.65 to −2.5, moderate −2.5 to −4, severe below −4. An entered z-score overrides the button. Obstruction severity comes from FEV1 when the FVC and/or TLC is normal (a low FEV1/FVC with a normal FEV1 is called mild); with a low FVC and no usable TLC it is left ungraded. Restriction is graded by TLC and mixed impairment by FEV1.
  • Optional rows (near limit, BMI, hemoglobin effect, VA, KCO, VA/TLC, effort, overall quality grade) are skipped by automatic row advance and by Next required. Amber highlights in the report preview, and the matching yellow items in Checks, mark wording that may need extra attention; highlights are display-only and never copied.
    • 
      
  • When DLCO is reduced and the Hb quality row says not adjusted (with no hemoglobin-effect choice), the interpretation adds that the DLCO has not been adjusted for hemoglobin and that anemia, if present, may contribute. Choosing the hemoglobin effect "Hgb not available" gives the unavailable-hemoglobin sentence instead.
    • 
      
  • In a spirometry-only study (lung volumes and DLCO both not performed) the empty LUNG VOLUMES and DIFFUSING CAPACITY sections are omitted. If only one of the two was skipped, that section still prints "Not performed."
    • 
      
  • The BMI button only adds an obesity note to a restrictive or mixed conclusion. Obesity is a likelier contributor at BMI above 40 kg/m²; at lower BMI it mainly lowers FRC and ERV.
    • 
      
  • Bronchodilator response = (post − pre) × 100 / predicted > 10% for FEV1 or FVC.
    • 
    

  






  

    
    
Settings

    

      
Lower limit of normal (z-score)
Used by the quick entry. ERS/ATS default is the 5th percentile (−1.645); the guideline allows −1.96 or −2.326 for low-risk screening if documented. The upper limit mirrors it (+).

      
      
Repeat the bronchodilator sentence in INTERPRETATION
Your template states it under both MECHANICS and INTERPRETATION.

      
      
Print “Not performed.” for sections marked X
Otherwise those sections are left out entirely.

      
      
Auto-advance after a single-choice pick
Moves to the next row so you can keep typing keys while you read.

      
      
Include DLCO severity in the text when chosen
e.g. “Mildly decreased.” instead of “Decreased.”
